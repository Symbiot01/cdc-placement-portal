CDC CLIENT BRIDGE

Your browser talks to ERP. Nothing else does.

This extension is a local bridge for the IIT Kharagpur CDC Placement Portal. It uses your existing ERP cookies so the portal can load roles, notices, and JDs without sending your password to any third-party server.

1

Install the bridge

Add this extension in Chrome. It only activates on the CDC Placement Portal and only contacts https://erp.iitkgp.ac.in.

2

Sign in locally

Enter roll number, password, and security answers in the portal. Those secrets stay in this browser. OTP still comes from official ERP email.

3

Apply from the portal

Browse open roles, read notices, open JDs, and apply or cancel with CV1 / CV2 / CV3. ERP session cookies never leave Chrome.

Unofficial student tool. Not affiliated with CDC, ERP, or IIT Kharagpur administration.
